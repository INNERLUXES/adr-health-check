# 1. Record architecture decisions

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The order platform is changed by three teams, and the reasons for its larger choices are spread over tickets and
calls.

## Decision

We will keep a short record of every architecturally significant decision in this folder, one file per decision, in
the format of Michael Nygard's post.

## Consequences

Every larger change starts with a record that the teams review like code.
